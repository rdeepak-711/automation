Practical Information

Quick-read guidance for scheduling, pacing, and general comfort during your visit.

Suggested visiting rhythm

Most visitors spend 1.5–2.5 hours at Sagrada Família. Structure your visit based on your ticket type and how much time you have.

  • Quick visit (1–1.5 hrs): self-guided entry with audio guide, basilica interior + museum
  • Standard visit (2 hrs): guided tour covering interior + symbolism + optional tower
  • Full experience (2.5+ hrs): guided tour + tower ascent + museum + exterior façade walk
  • Photography visit: book early morning for east-facing stained glass, or late afternoon for west-side golden light

Helpful things to know in advance

A few practical details that help the day go smoother, especially for first-time visitors to Barcelona.

  • Wear comfortable shoes — you will be standing and walking for 1–2+ hours
  • Check the dress code — shoulders and knees must be covered
  • Arrive via Metro L2 or L5 for the simplest route
  • Review the entrance guide to know which façade to approach
  • Save the basilica map on your phone before arriving
  • Leave large bags and luggage at your hotel — there are no lockers on site

Visitor tips worth keeping in mind

Small details that experienced visitors wish they had known before their first visit.

  • Arrive 15 minutes before your booked time slot
  • Morning visits (9 AM) have the smallest crowds
  • The audio guide is included with most entry tickets — don’t skip it
  • Tower access is via elevator up and narrow spiral stairs down — not suitable for vertigo or claustrophobia
  • Personal photography is allowed but tripods and flash are not
  • Budget extra time if you want to explore the exterior façades and nearby park

Cancellations & refund policy

Understand the refund terms before you book to avoid surprises.

  • Most Sagrada Família tickets are non-refundable
  • Some tickets offer free cancellation up to 24 hours before — check the specific listing
  • Latecomers are denied entry with no refund
  • Sagrada Família remains open rain or shine — weather is not grounds for a refund
  • Time slots cannot be changed once booked on most ticket types

Things to Know Before You Book

Key reminders for a hassle-free visit.

🎫 Book online in advance — Walk-up tickets are often unavailable. Secure your time slot online, especially during peak season (April–October).
👗 Dress code enforced — Shoulders and knees must be covered. Non-compliant visitors will be denied entry with no refund.
⏰ Arrive 15 minutes early — Entry is at your booked time slot only. Security screening takes time, and latecomers are not admitted.
🗼 Tower access is separate — Towers require a specific ticket add-on. Not wheelchair accessible — elevator up, narrow spiral stairs down.
📸 Photography allowed — Personal photos permitted. Tripods, flash, and commercial photography require advance permission.
🔄 Cancellation policy — Most tickets are non-refundable. Some offer 24-hour free cancellation — always check the terms before booking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions visitors ask when planning a trip to Sagrada Família.

Sagrada Família is open daily. Hours vary by season: 9 AM–6 PM in winter, 9 AM–7 PM in spring/autumn, and 9 AM–8 PM in summer. Last entry is 30 minutes before closing. Special hours apply on 25–26 December and 1–6 January.
The easiest way is Metro L2 (Purple) or L5 (Blue) to Sagrada Família station — the exit leads directly to the basilica. Buses 19, 33, 34, 43, 44, and 50 also stop nearby. The Nativity Façade entrance is on Carrer de la Marina.
Shoulders and knees must be covered. Avoid transparent clothing, swimwear, and hats inside the basilica. Comfortable walking shoes are recommended. Visitors in non-compliant attire may be denied entry.
Early morning (9 AM at opening) and late afternoon (after 4 PM) are the quietest times. Weekdays are less crowded than weekends. November through February is the quietest season overall.
The basilica interior is fully wheelchair accessible. However, the towers are NOT accessible to wheelchair users — access is via narrow elevators and spiral staircases only.
A self-guided visit takes about 1–1.5 hours. Guided tours run 1.5–2 hours. Add 30 minutes for tower access. Budget 2–2.5 hours total for a complete experience including the museum and exterior.
Small bags and backpacks are allowed but will be screened at security. Large luggage and oversized bags are not permitted, and there are no lockers on site. Travel light when visiting.
Yes, several parking garages are located within a short walk of the basilica. Street parking is also available but limited. Public transport (Metro L2 or L5) is generally the easier and cheaper option.

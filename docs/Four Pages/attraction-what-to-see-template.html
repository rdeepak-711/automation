Top Highlights at Sagrada Família

The headline sights and experiences most visitors want to see first.

Must-See Interior Light & Colour

The Interior & Stained Glass

Gaudí designed the interior as a forest of branching columns bathed in rainbow light from floor-to-ceiling stained glass windows. Morning light illuminates warm reds and oranges on the east side; afternoon brings cool blues and greens on the west. This is the single most breathtaking experience at Sagrada Família and the reason most visitors book early morning slots.

Panoramic Views Iconic Add-On Ticket

The Towers

Ascend the Nativity or Passion tower by elevator for breathtaking panoramic views of Barcelona, the Mediterranean, and Montjuïc. The spiral staircases down are an architectural experience in themselves. Tower access requires a separate ticket add-on and is not wheelchair accessible.

How to Choose What to See

A quick guide based on your interests and visit style.

Best for First-Time Visitors

  • Start with the interior and stained glass — the single most impressive experience
  • Walk both the Nativity and Passion façades to see the contrast
  • Add tower access for panoramic Barcelona views
  • Visit the crypt and museum to understand Gaudí’s vision
Recommended: Guided Tour + Tower Access — an expert guide explains the symbolism you’d miss on your own, plus tower views are unforgettable.

Best for Architecture & History Lovers

  • Deep-dive into the interior columns and vaults designed as a stone forest
  • Study the Nativity Façade carvings — Gaudí’s most personal work
  • Explore the museum for original models and construction drawings
  • Read about the Tower of Jesus and the ongoing 140-year build
Recommended: Private Tour — a dedicated guide adapts to your interests and goes deeper into Gaudí’s methods and symbolism.

Best for Photographers

  • Book an early morning slot for warm east-facing stained glass light
  • Or visit late afternoon for cool blue and green west-side tones
  • The tower spiral staircases create striking geometric compositions
  • Both façades offer dramatic exterior detail shots
Tip: Personal photography is allowed. Tripods and flash are not permitted inside.

Best for Spiritual Visitors

  • Attend Holy Mass — free and held regularly
  • Visit the altar and apse to see Gaudí’s sacred design intent
  • Pay respects at Gaudí’s tomb in the crypt
  • The stained glass creates a contemplative atmosphere throughout the day
Note: Sagrada Família is an active place of worship. Respectful dress and behaviour are required.

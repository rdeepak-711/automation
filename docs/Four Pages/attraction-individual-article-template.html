Opera Garnier Entry Ticket: Price, What’s Included & Practical Tips

Everything you need to know about buying the €25 Opera Garnier entry ticket — what’s included, what isn’t, and how to visit

An Opera Garnier (Palais Garnier) entry ticket costs €25 per adult in 2026. The ticket gives self-guided access to the public areas including the Grand Staircase, Grand Foyer, and Library-Museum. Auditorium access is not guaranteed — it depends on the rehearsal schedule on the day.

Opera Garnier entry tickets are €25 per adult and sell out on weekends — booking online saves you both the queue and the disappointment of a sold-out desk. The ticket covers a self-guided visit to the building’s public areas: the legendary Grand Staircase, the gold-and-marble Grand Foyer, the Avant-Foyer, the decorative salons, and the Library-Museum of the Opéra.

What the standard ticket does not include: a guided tour, the audio guide (€6.50 extra), or guaranteed access to the main auditorium with Marc Chagall’s painted ceiling — that space is frequently closed for rehearsals without notice. For most visitors, the €25 ticket provides 90 minutes to two hours of genuinely extraordinary architecture.

What Does the €25 Entry Ticket Include?

The €25 Opera Garnier entry ticket includes self-guided access to the Grand Staircase, Grand Foyer, Avant-Foyer, Moon and Sun Salons, Rotonde des abonnés, Rotonde du Glacier, and the Library-Museum of the Opéra. Any temporary exhibitions running at the time of your visit are also included.

The building was never just a performance venue — Charles Garnier designed it as a stage for Parisian society itself. The Grand Staircase, built from white Carrara marble with balustrades of red and green marble, was engineered so audience members could see and be seen as they arrived. The entry ticket puts you on those steps.

Insider Tip

Weekend entry sells out by Wednesday. If you’re visiting on a Saturday or Sunday, check availability on Tuesday and book immediately. For same-day visits, the ticket desk opens at 10:00 — arrive by 09:45 and join the queue before it forms.

Is the Auditorium Included?

No — access to the main auditorium (home to Marc Chagall’s famous painted ceiling) is not guaranteed with the standard entry ticket. The auditorium is frequently closed to visitors for rehearsals or technical preparations without advance notice. On days when it is open, visitors can enter briefly, but this is not guaranteed.

Before You Arrive

  • Metro: Opéra (lines 3, 7, 8) — 2-minute walk from the main entrance
  • Photography: Permitted in all public areas; flash off near exhibits
  • Timed entry: Arrive within 15 minutes of your booked slot
  • Accessibility: Lift access available; contact venue for specific requirements

Ready to Visit Opera Garnier?

The €25 entry ticket is the right choice for independent visitors who want to explore the public rooms at their own pace. You’ll have 90 minutes to two hours to move through some of the most elaborately decorated spaces in Paris.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to print my Opera Garnier ticket?

No — mobile tickets are accepted. Show the QR code on your phone at the entrance. It is advisable to download the ticket to your phone before arriving in case of poor mobile signal at the venue.

How long does a self-guided visit to Opera Garnier take?

Most visitors spend 90 minutes to 2 hours on a self-guided visit. If you add the audio guide and spend time in the Library-Museum, budget 2 to 2.5 hours. There is no time limit once you are inside.

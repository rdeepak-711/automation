Section C: Content Categories
All pages on the site fall into these three navigation categories. Every page must be assigned to one.

Category 1: Tickets & Tours
Purpose: Transactional and commercial investigation pages. These are MONEY PAGES.
What belongs here: Ticket buying guides, tour comparisons, skip-the-line guides, combo ticket guides, specific tour pages.
Visitor mindset: "I've decided to visit — help me choose the right ticket/tour and book it."
{Menu label}  Tickets & Tours

Category 2: Plan Your Visit
Purpose: High-value informational pages that answer practical planning questions. FUNNEL PAGES — they move readers toward tickets.
What belongs here: Best time to visit, opening hours, how to get there, tips, what to wear, accessibility, nearby food, where to stay.
Visitor mindset: "I'm planning my visit — help me prepare so I have the best experience."
{Menu label}  Plan Your Visit

Category 3: What to See
Purpose: Informational and authority-building pages about the attraction's contents. Build topical authority and capture mid-funnel traffic.
What belongs here: Highlights, must-see areas, history, architecture, exhibitions, events, photo spots.
Visitor mindset: "I want to know what's inside and what I shouldn't miss."
{Menu label}  What to See

Additional Fixed Pages

- Privacy Policy / Terms
- Blog index (if posts don't fit the three categories)

Section E: Product Display Rules
These rules govern how products from the catalog appear across the site:

Menu Integration
- Products marked "Show in Menu: Yes" appear in a dropdown under the Tickets & Tours nav item
- Menu order follows Priority ranking (1 = first)
- Each menu item links directly to the affiliate link (not to a page on the site)
- Menu CTA button label: "Book Tickets" — always the rightmost nav item, styled as a button

Comparison Table Rules
- The main tickets page (/tickets/) must include a comparison table showing ALL products
- Columns: Product Name | Price (Adult) | What's Included | Best For | Book Link
- Sort by Priority (1 = top row)
- Use a highlight/recommended badge on the Priority 1 product

CTA Rules
- Default CTA (Quick Answer boxes, sticky bars, generic placements) always promotes Priority 1 product
- Section-specific CTAs promote the most relevant product (e.g., guided tours section → guided tour product)
- Always include the price in CTAs
- Sticky bottom bar: "{Short Label} from {Price} — {Key Selling Point} → Book Now"

Contextual Mention Rules
- When article mentions a topic mapping to a specific product, link to that product inline
- Never link the same product more than 3 times in one article
- Space affiliate links at least 200 words apart

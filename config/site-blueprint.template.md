## Section E: Product Display Rules
<!-- These are fixed rules — do not change them unless you have a specific reason. -->

**Menu Integration**
- Products with `Show in Menu: Yes` appear as a dropdown under the Tickets & Tours nav item
- Menu order follows Priority ranking (1 = first)
- Each menu item links directly to the affiliate link (not an internal page)
- Rightmost nav item is always a "Book Tickets" button linking to the Priority 1 product

**Comparison Table** (required on `/tickets/` page)
- Shows ALL products from the catalog
- Columns: Product Name | Price (Adult) | What's Included | Best For | Book Link
- Sorted by Priority (1 = top row)
- Recommended badge on Priority 1 product

**CTA Rules**
- Default CTA (Quick Answer boxes, sticky bar, generic placements) → Priority 1 product always
- Section-specific CTAs → most relevant product for that section
- Always include the price in CTAs
- Sticky bar format: `{Short Label} from {Price} — {Key Selling Point} → Book Now`

**Contextual Link Rules**
- Link inline when article mentions a topic that maps to a specific product
- Max 3 links per product per article
- Minimum 200 words between affiliate links
